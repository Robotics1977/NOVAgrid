# 🤖 Alfred 5 — The Butler Robot
**Platform:** Raspberry Pi 5 + Wheelchair Motors  
**Role:** Full-size indoor butler robot  
**OS / Stack:** Ubuntu 24.04 + ROS 2 Jazzy  
**Communication:** ROS 2 (local + networked with NOVA)  
**Status:** Future project

---

## 📘 Overview
Alfred 5 is the ultimate goal of the Alfred series — a full-sized robotic butler capable of:

- answering the door  
- carrying small items  
- vacuuming or sweeping  
- interacting with NOVA and the house environment  

He is inspired by:

- Alfred (Batman’s butler)  
- Johnny 5  
- Lurch (Addams Family)  
- A minion-in-a-butler-suit aesthetic  

Alfred 5 is the culmination of everything learned from Alfred 1.  
He is **ambitious**, **complex**, and the centerpiece of the NOVAgrid robotics fleet.

## 🔩 Hardware (Planned)
| Component | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| **Raspberry Pi 5 + SSD** | Main compute |
| **Electric wheelchair motors** | Powerful full-scale locomotion |
| **IBT-2 H-bridge motor drivers** | High-current motor control |
| **360° LIDAR** | Mapping + navigation |
| **Stereo camera OR depth camera** | Perception |
| **Large LiFePO4 pack** | Main battery |
| **Modular chassis** | Metal frame with easy access panels |
| **Optional arm** | 1–2 DOF actuator for grabbing objects |
