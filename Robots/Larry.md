# 🤖 Larry — Rover 1
**Platform:** Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W  
**Role:** Lightweight mini rover  
**OS / Stack:** ROS 2 micro / minimal ROS 2  
**Communication:** ROS2-Lite or MQTT  
**Status:** Planned

---

## 📘 Overview
Larry is the first of the two “NKOK chassis” rovers — small, nimble, fast to test, and perfect for exploring lightweight robotics systems.

Larry is meant to:

- act as a portable mapping / exploration rover  
- test micro-ROS or lightweight ROS2 nodes  
- perform simple autonomy experiments
